Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy explains what data Onifide collects, why, where it is stored, and what your rights are. We have tried to write it in a readable way rather than in impenetrable legal jargon — if any passage remains unclear, contact us (see §9).

1. Who is responsible for your data

Onifide is published by Mohamed Djamil, sole proprietorship (SIREN 108339714, SIRET of the main establishment 108339714 00017), based in Toulouse, France. He is the data controller for your data within the meaning of the GDPR — the same structure as the publisher's first product, TallUp.

2. What Onifide is not

Before we talk about data: Onifide is neither a religious authority nor a medical or psychological service. Dream interpretations are reflection tools generated by AI from a documentary corpus, never an absolute truth, a diagnosis or a prediction. See the terms of use for details.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon, in the European Union (AWS region eu-central-1, Frankfurt, Germany). Unlike an app that would keep everything solely on your device, Onifide needs a server to generate interpretations and sync your journal across your devices — this is a deliberate architecture choice, not an oversight.

The only exception: generated dream images (Premium/Ultime) are stored on Cloudflare R2 (file storage), in a space created under European Union jurisdiction. They are identified there by technical identifiers, never by your email or by the text of the dream.

5. The third parties your data passes through

We use only the strict minimum, and never to target you with advertising based on the content of your dreams (see §6).

  • OpenAI (United States): the text of your dreams is sent to OpenAI to generate the similarity vectors (embeddings) that make it possible to find the relevant content in our documentary library, and then to write the interpretation itself. The text of a dream is also sent once to extract the labels that feed the evolving psychological profile (figures, motifs, symbols); the aggregation that follows is done on our side, with no further sending. By contrast, dream image generation never receives the raw text: it starts solely from the symbols already extracted, turned into a short visual description. OpenAI processes this data on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. Data sent through OpenAI's API is not used to train its models (this is the default behavior of the API, unlike the consumer ChatGPT) and is retained by OpenAI for a maximum of 30 days for abuse-detection purposes, then deleted.
  • Sentry (error tracking): the app's technical errors are reported to Sentry so that we can fix bugs. Free-text fields (dream content, mood notes, exercise transcripts) are explicitly filtered (scrubbed) before anything is sent — they must never appear in a Sentry event.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage): hosts only the generated dream images (see §4). Cloudflare never receives the text of your dreams or any other data from your journal — only the image file itself. These images are deleted from R2 at the same time as your account.
  • System speech recognition (Apple / Google): if you dictate a dream instead of typing it, the app first uses your device's own speech recognition, offline — in that case the audio never leaves your phone. When your device cannot transcribe offline (depending on its model, its operating system version or the language), the transcription is performed by the system's speech recognition service (Apple or Google), to which the audio is then transmitted for the duration of the transcription. On our side, we only receive the transcribed text, never the audio. Typing your dream on the keyboard involves none of these services.
  • Apple / Google: for "Sign in with Apple/Google" authentication if you choose that option, and for managing subscriptions through the stores.
  • RevenueCat: manages the state of your subscription between the stores and our server. Receives a subscriber identifier and the purchase information passed on by the store, never the content of your journal.
  • Brevo (email delivery, European Union): delivers only the service emails you trigger yourself — the code to verify your email address and the password reset code. Brevo receives your email address and the content of that message, never the content of your journal. We send no newsletters or marketing emails.

We never sell your data to anyone.

6. Advertising and no targeting based on the content of your dreams

Onifide embeds no advertising SDK. The free tier is a trial (one first dream offered), not an ad-funded tier — so there is no ad network receiving anything at all.

If advertising were ever to be introduced one day, the following commitment would remain in full: no ad network would receive the content of your dreams, your mood notes, the transcripts of your exercises, or any signal computed from that data. There will never be any "smart targeting" based on what you confide to your journal.

7. How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (available directly in Settings), your data (dreams, interpretations, dream images, mood, exercises, psychological profiles, and the account itself) is deleted immediately and permanently from our active database and from image storage — this is not a deactivation, the deletion is real. The technical restore history kept by our database host then expires automatically, within 7 days at most. Error events (Sentry) are kept for a maximum of 90 days, for security and debugging purposes only.

If you asked for the free guide and the tips emails, your consent is kept until you unsubscribe (link in every email, or the switch in the app's settings) or until you delete your account, which stops every email at once. These emails go through our email provider (Brevo) without building any contact list there. An address left on our website without an account is deleted on request (§9).

8. Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR (and for all our users, wherever they are, not only in the European Union), you can at any time:

  • Export your data: Settings → "Export my data" generates a complete export (dreams, interpretations, mood, exercises, psychological profiles) in JSON format.
  • Delete your account: Settings → "Delete my account" — a permanent and irreversible action.
  • Access, rectify or object to the processing of your data: contact us (§9).
  • Lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés, the French data protection authority, www.cnil.fr) if you believe your rights are not being respected.

10. Minimum age

Onifide can be used from the age of 15 — the age of digital majority in France, below which the GDPR requires joint parental consent for the processing of data. Since dream content is involuntary, it can sometimes bring up difficult themes that the user did not choose; the app handles them with restraint and never puts them forward. We do not knowingly collect data belonging to children under 15.

11. In case of psychological distress

Onifide is neither an emergency service nor a helpline. If a dream or an exercise brings up real distress, talk to a healthcare professional or a helpline in your country — and in case of immediate danger, contact the emergency services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Any substantial change will be notified to you in the app before it takes effect.
